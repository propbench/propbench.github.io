Start here: sixty mornings, five desks each

Welcome. This is a small paper about prop desks, written the way you would talk to a friend who is about to pay a fee. No trophy, no secret code, no promise of a payout.

The series has one post for each of its 60 days. Five desks, pros and cons. Each post is published in English. The category English is the archive; scroll it when you want yesterday.

The chairs are desks with a public life: HUKProp, FTMO, The5ers, FundedNext, Funding Pips, E8, Alpha Capital, City Traders Imperium, Funded Trading Plus, Maven, Blue Guardian, FunderPro, For Traders, Topstep, Apex, Take Profit Trader, and Earn2Trade. Futures and forex are labeled as different sports.
