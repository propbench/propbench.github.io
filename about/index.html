About PropBench

PropBench is a comparison site for proprietary trading firms. It helps readers review firms’ publicly stated rules and costs before choosing a challenge.

Our articles bring key details—such as evaluation rules, fees, drawdown limits, profit splits, and payout terms—into one place. We explain what firms publish and highlight what readers should verify on their official websites. PropBench is a comparison resource, not a ranking or recommendation.

Firm terms can change. This site is for general information, not financial advice. Check the firm’s current official terms before paying. PropBench is available in multiple languages and does not use affiliate links.
