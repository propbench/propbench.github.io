About this bench

PropBench is a morning bench, not a ranking. Each day we pull five chairs up to the same table: what the desk is for, what is pleasant about it, and what should make you pause. Every post is published in English.

The chairs change every day across the 60 days of the series. That is sixty mornings. After that, this series is finished. Nothing here wakes up on its own and writes day sixty-one. The archive is simply the English category.

HUKProp is not a trophy, not a paid rank, and not a hint to buy. For HUKProp, as for every desk, we quote its own page and we also say what that page did not show.

We did not freeze a fee for the older houses. Sales move, and two blogs rarely agree. Where we describe a habit — static loss, trailing loss, a combine, a syllabus — treat it as a personality, then confirm it on the plan you would actually pay for. The only prices printed as prices are the ones HUKProp itself was showing on 2 November 2026.

There are no affiliate links on this site. A link is just the front door.
