Prop Firm Comparison: FTMO, The5ers, FundedNext, Funding Pips, Earn2Trade

Day 4 of 60

Today’s five desks

Where the money story points

Every desk has a story about where a payout comes from. Some say a profit split after you pass. Some say a cap per cycle. One gold desk, on the days it sits here, says a multiplier on profit and then, in another sentence, the single best trade. Today’s storytellers are FTMO, The5ers, FundedNext, Funding Pips and Earn2Trade. Believe the sentence in the rules, not the sentence in the banner.

Today is CFDs beside futures. Your platform habit will make one of them feel like home and the other like a foreign kitchen.

The desks, one by one

FTMO

Forex and CFDs · Prague, around since 2015

The old public rulebook. A static loss ceiling on the classic challenge, not a trailing one.

FTMO is the Prague desk people already knew before prop ads filled every feed. The famous path is two steps, and there is a one-step path too. This is forex and CFDs, not Chicago futures. On the standard challenge the max-loss habit is a fixed ceiling: your profit does not drag the floor up behind you. It is rarely the cheapest door, which is exactly why bargain hunters usually walk past it.

✔ What is easy to like

  • The rulebook has been public for years, so a breach can be argued against a text, not a mood.
  • Familiar rooms: MetaTrader 4 and 5, cTrader, and TradingView, depending on the program.
  • A static ceiling is easier to picture if trailing drawdown makes you nervous.

⚠ What should make you pause

  • The entry fee usually sits above the younger desks.
  • Minimum trading days mean one lucky ticket does not pass you.
  • News rules and the country list have moved before. Read them the day you pay.

🔎 Look again before you pay: Fee, split, and whether your country is still on the list.

The5ers

Forex · Around since 2016

Built around growing an account, not only surviving a two-step postcard.

The5ers has been around since about 2016, with more than one way in: a bootcamp-style grind and a higher-stakes challenge, depending on the year you look. The story they like to tell is scaling, and a split that can rise once the account grows up. Some programs are kinder about the clock than a classic 30-day sprint. The part people skip is the payout cap per cycle. A pretty split with a low cap is a smaller paycheck than the banner suggests.

✔ What is easy to like

  • A long public life, which is rarer than a loud discount.
  • Scaling is the point of the house, not a footnote.
  • Some paths do not trap you in a short calendar.

⚠ What should make you pause

  • A payout cap can make a high split less generous than it sounds.
  • Several programs means you can buy the wrong personality by accident.
  • Forex only. If you wanted equity index futures, this is the wrong room.

🔎 Look again before you pay: Which program you clicked, its cap, and the current split table.

Visit site · the5ers.com

FundedNext

Forex and CFDs · UAE, around since 2022

A busy shop. Stellar-style challenges, frequent sales, rules that get edited.

FundedNext showed up around 2022 out of the UAE and got big fast. Stellar is the name you will see on the two-step and the one-step. The sticker price moves with promotions, so any number in an old article is a souvenir. News and consistency rules are not the same on every plan. It is a fine desk if you like a modern CFD challenge and you are willing to re-read the PDF after every sale banner.

✔ What is easy to like

  • More than one speed: one step or two, if you know which you bought.
  • Promos can make the door cheaper than the quiet old houses.
  • A large trader crowd, so public complaints and payouts are easy to go looking for.

⚠ What should make you pause

  • The rule page moves. A screenshot from last month can be wrong.
  • A sale is not a personality. The drawdown type still decides your week.
  • Younger than FTMO. Four busy years is not the same as a decade.

🔎 Look again before you pay: The exact Stellar plan: daily loss, max loss, news, and today's fee.

Visit site · fundednext.com

Funding Pips

Forex and CFDs · Around since 2022

Several shapes, including a one-step, and a split they like to advertise high.

Funding Pips is one of the CFD names that grew up in the same noisy years as FundedNext. You will find more than one evaluation shape, including a one-step flex-style plan. The advertised split is often high, which is pleasant and also a reason to read the fine lines twice. Prohibited strategies are where people get surprised: what felt like normal trading on a broker can be a breach here. Price tags move with codes.

✔ What is easy to like

  • A one-step option if two rounds feel like homework.
  • High advertised splits, when the plan you picked really says that.
  • Low published entry points during promotions.

⚠ What should make you pause

  • The banned-strategy list is not optional reading.
  • A young firm. Public memory is shorter than a decade-old desk.
  • Codes and add-ons make the checkout a different price from the homepage hero.

🔎 Look again before you pay: Prohibited strategies, consistency, and the fee after the code.

Visit site · fundingpips.com

Earn2Trade

Futures, with a syllabus beside the eval · Older futures-education brand

More syllabus, less coupon. A gauntlet you walk, not a banner you click.

Earn2Trade is the futures desk that still smells like a course. The Gauntlet-style evaluation sits next to lessons, which some traders find patronizing and others find like a railing on a stair. There is often a daily loss in the conversation, not only a trail. It will not win a discount war with Apex, and it is not trying to. Come here if you want structure. Do not come here if you wanted a one-day gold challenge with a tiny lot cap.

✔ What is easy to like

  • A syllabus if you actually want to be taught, not only funded.
  • Older than the coupon generation of futures ads.
  • Daily loss, where it applies, is a fence you can see.

⚠ What should make you pause

  • The course tone annoys traders who already have a system.
  • Not cheap-feeling next to a futures sale week.
  • Still the wrong sport for a spot-gold, one-day, MetaTrader habit.

🔎 Look again before you pay: Which gauntlet, whether a daily loss applies, and the current tuition.

Visit site · earn2trade.com
