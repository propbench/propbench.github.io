This is not financial advice, not a signal, and not a promise that anyone gets paid. Prop desks change fees, countries, news rules, and drawdowns. A sentence that was fair on 2 November 2026 can be wrong next month.

You are trading under someone else’s rulebook, often on a simulated account, and you can lose the fee. Nothing here says you will pass.

Do not limit comparisons between prop firms to the challenge price, advertised account size, or profit split. Details such as refund terms, daily and overall drawdown rules, time limits, payout conditions, how drawdown is calculated, and how trading violations are handled can create important differences between firms.

Before choosing a firm, check its official rules and latest updates directly on its website. Do not make your final decision based solely on advertisements, rankings, or one person’s experience. Even firms with seemingly similar terms may differ significantly in their implementation details and policies.

The goal of this comparison is not to find the firm with the largest account or the cheapest challenge, but to understand the terms, limitations, and risks more clearly so you can make a more informed choice about firms that may offer a better chance of making a profit. Ultimately, do not choose any firm without independently reviewing its rules and considering how well it fits your trading style and circumstances.
